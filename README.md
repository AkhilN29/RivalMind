# RivalMind – AI Competitor Intelligence Copilot
**BITSoM Product Management Capstone · Apr 2026 – Aug 2026**

## The problem
B2B sellers spend anywhere from 20 minutes to 3 days researching each prospect meeting, jumping between 6+ tools to piece together competitor and account context.

## Discovery
- Ran 5 Jobs-to-be-Done interviews with B2B sellers across SaaS, aviation, manufacturing and digital marketing
- Every interviewee ranked **accuracy over speed**, so trust became the core product bet

## The solution
An AI copilot that generates a sourced prospect brief in minutes, with source citations and confidence scores built into the MVP so sellers can trust what they read.

## Key product decisions
- Prioritized 25+ feature ideas into an 8-capability MVP using RICE and MoSCoW
- Set a **≥95% groundedness launch gate**: every AI claim must be backed by a source
